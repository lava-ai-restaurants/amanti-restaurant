What To Expect

A neighborhood Italian restaurant that still feels designed.

Come for food you understand quickly: focaccia, burrata, pizza, pasta, fish, wine, and a few larger plates when dinner turns into a longer night.

The room is warm and polished, but the point is simple: make Amanti useful enough for Tuesday and good-looking enough for Saturday.
