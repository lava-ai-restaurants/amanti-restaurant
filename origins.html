Food & Origins

The Night Starts At The Table.

Amanti takes the Italian supper club seriously: aperitivo energy, regional ingredients, handmade-style pasta, coastal plates, wood-fired attitude, and hospitality that gets warmer as the room fills.

The Concept

Italian food with memory. Scottsdale energy with polish.

Amanti was built around a simple idea: dinner should feel seductive, social, and generous. The food pulls from Italy's great dining rituals: antipasti for the table, pasta with depth, pizza with blistered edges, seafood with citrus and olive oil, grilled meats with herbs, and dolce that keeps the conversation alive.

Italian Signals

Every dish carries a place, a ritual, or a reason.

Emilia-Romagna

Mortadella, Parmigiano Reggiano, rich pastas, and the comfort of a table that wants more bread.

Campania

San Marzano tomatoes, bufala, basil, olive oil, and the clean pleasure of a proper Margherita.

Friuli & The North

Prosciutto San Daniele, porcini, truffle, alpine cheese, and the deeper winter side of Italian cooking.

Calabria & The Coast

Chili, nduja, shrimp, octopus, branzino, lemon, herbs, and plates that taste bright before they taste heavy.

The Ritual

Aperitivo. Antipasti. Pasta. Wine. One more drink.

The Amanti rhythm is intentionally Italian: start lighter, share often, let the table fill, then let the night stretch. The bar is not an accessory to dinner. It is part of the concept.

How The Menu Works

Built for sharing, pacing, and a table that orders in waves.

Bright First

Crudo, shrimp, burrata, olives, bread, citrus, herbs, and cold plates that wake up the table.

Craveable Center

Pizza, pasta, cheese, sauce, truffle, ragu, and plates that make the table slow down.

Fire & Finish

Branzino, octopus, tagliata, filet, lamb, roasted vegetables, potatoes, and sauces with backbone.

Why Amanti

Because the best Italian restaurants are not only about food. They are about wanting to stay.

The name means lovers. The room is designed for them: lovers of dinner, wine, music, cocktails, late tables, celebrations, and that specific moment when a meal stops being a meal and becomes the night.
